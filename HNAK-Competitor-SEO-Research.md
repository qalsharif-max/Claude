# Competitive SEO Positioning — HNAK.com vs. KSA E-Commerce Leaders
### Deep-Research Brief: Keyword & Backlink Analysis

**Prepared:** 8 June 2026
**Subject:** HNAK.com (online arm of Al Musbah Group) vs. Amazon.sa, Noon, Jarir, eXtra, Namshi
**Method:** Multi-source web research (5 parallel research angles), cross-checked across SimilarWeb, Semrush/Ahrefs public data, market reports, and SEO industry sources. Claims are **tiered by confidence** because much precise keyword/backlink data sits behind paid tools.

> **Confidence legend:** 🟢 High (multiple independent sources agree) · 🟡 Medium (single credible source / reasonable estimate) · 🔴 Low (single weak source, oddly specific, or internally inconsistent — treat as directional only).

---

## 1. Headline Finding

🟢 **HNAK is a genuine but sub-scale SEO player.** Across SimilarWeb, Semrush, Ahrefs, and every major KSA e-commerce market report (ResearchAndMarkets, Mordor, GlobeNewswire), HNAK **does not appear** in top-site rankings, and **no public free-tool metrics** (Domain Rating, Authority Score, organic keyword count, traffic) are available for the domain. The competitors all have rich public profiles; HNAK's absence is itself the signal — it operates well below the visibility threshold these tools track.
*Sources: [Semrush KSA top retail](https://www.semrush.com/website/top/saudi-arabia/e-commerce-and-retail/), [ResearchAndMarkets 2025-2033](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/04/21/3064772/28124/en/Saudi-Arabia-Ecommerce-Market-Report-and-Company-Analysis-2025-2033-Featuring-Amazon-eBay-Emaar-Properties-Namshi-Noon-Alibaba-Jarir-Marketing-and-Carrefour.html), [Scamadviser HNAK](https://www.scamadviser.com/check-website/hnak.com)*

**Strategic implication:** HNAK cannot win on authority. Its realistic battleground is **Arabic-language, long-tail, localized, and category-niche** queries where the giants under-optimize.

## 3. Keyword Footprint & Where the Demand Is

### Competitor positioning
- 🟡 **Noon** leans on organic search as a *top* traffic driver (~41% of desktop visits), running a promotion/discount-led visibility strategy. **Amazon.sa** is **direct-traffic dominant (~42%)** — brand-led, with organic secondary. *Source: [Semrush noon overview](https://www.semrush.com/website/noon.com/overview/), [SimilarWeb amazon.sa](https://www.similarweb.com/website/amazon.sa/)*
- 🟢 **Jarir + eXtra own the electronics/transactional keyword real estate** (smartphones, laptops, gaming, appliances) — backed by same-day delivery and physical-store trust. *Source: [ClickPost KSA e-commerce](https://www.clickpost.ai/blog/best-ecommerce-companies-in-saudi-arabia)*
- 🟢 **Namshi owns fashion** as a vertical specialist (1,200+ brands). *Source: [Istizada MENA top sites](https://istizada.com/blog/top-25-middle-east-ecommerce-websites-in-arabic)*

> ⚠️ **Traffic numbers conflict heavily across sources** (e.g., Amazon.sa reported anywhere from ~12M to ~61M monthly visits; Noon ~8.6M to ~26M; Jarir ~5.7M to ~25M). These mix "total" vs "monthly" vs different months and tools. **Do not cite specific visit counts as fact** — the reliable signal is *rank order* (Amazon.sa ≳ Noon ≫ Jarir ≈ eXtra ≫ Namshi), not the absolute figures.

### The search-demand structure (this is the opportunity)
- 🟢 **Google holds ~95–97% of KSA search.** *Source: [Go-Globe Arabic SEO](https://www.go-globe.com/seo-guide-for-arabic-world-heres-how-to-start/)*
- 🟢 **KSA is the most Arabic-dominant Gulf market.** Most product, review, and query content is Arabic (often Saudi dialect). English and Arabic require **separate keyword strategies** — do not machine-translate. *Sources: [Go-Globe](https://www.go-globe.com/seo-guide-for-arabic-world-heres-how-to-start/), [Upraise Arabic SEO](https://upraiseseo.com/arabic-seo-best-practices/)*
- 🟡 ~68% of Saudi consumers prefer searching in Arabic; many code-switch EN/AR within a session. *Source: [23hublab](https://23hublab.com/arabic-vs-english-search-behavior-in-saudi-arabia/) — single source, treat as directional.*
- 🔴 "79% of KSA stores use Google Translate for Arabic" and "Arabic voice search +67% YoY" — single weak sources; **unverified**, cite cautiously. *Sources: [seosaudiarabia.company](https://seosaudiarabia.company/blog/arabic-seo-guide-saudi-arabia.html), [23hublab](https://23hublab.com/arabic-vs-english-search-behavior-in-saudi-arabia/)*

**Net:** The leaders are strongest in **English brand/transactional** terms and well-funded paid/promo. The **native-Arabic long-tail is comparatively under-served** — and that is precisely where a mid-tier site can rank.

## 4. Market Context (sizing the prize)

- 🟢 KSA e-commerce is a large, fast-growing market — credible estimates cluster around **~USD 21–31B in 2024–2026, growing ~12% CAGR** toward USD 50B+ by the early 2030s. (Exact figures vary by analyst.) *Sources: [Mordor](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/saudi-arabia-ecommerce-market), [Grand View](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/horizon/outlook/e-commerce-market/saudi-arabia)*
- 🟢 **Mobile-first & high connectivity:** ~78–80% of e-commerce activity is on mobile; near-universal internet penetration. *Sources: [23hublab](https://23hublab.com/arabic-vs-english-search-behavior-in-saudi-arabia/), [Safha](https://safha.sa/en/seo-for-e-commerce-in-saudi-arabia/), [GlobeNewswire Vision 2030](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/22/3223626/28124/en/Saudi-Arabia-Ecommerce-Market-Trends-and-Expansion-Potential-2026-2031-Vision-2030-Boosts-Saudi-Ecommerce-with-99-Internet-Penetration-78-5G-Coverage.html)*
- 🟢 **Fragmented by vertical:** marketplaces (Amazon.sa, Noon) lead breadth; specialists own categories (Jarir/eXtra electronics, Namshi fashion). Mid-tier players win by **category depth, not breadth.** *Source: [BusinessWire/ResearchAndMarkets](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241104360540/en/Saudi-Arabia-Ecommerce-Market-Report-and-Company-Analysis-2024-2032-Homegrown-Platforms-Like-Noon-and-Jarir.com-Dominate-the-$41.59-Billion-Market---ResearchAndMarkets.com)*
- 🟡 **Seasonal peaks** (Ramadan/Eid, White Friday, back-to-school) drive major spikes; evening/post-iftar mobile shopping dominates Ramadan. *Source: [23hublab](https://23hublab.com/arabic-vs-english-search-behavior-in-saudi-arabia/)*
- 🔴 Oddly precise Ramadan-2026 stats ("Noon +45% orders, Amazon +38%", "65B SAR spend") trace to low-authority outlets (thesauditimes.net, themiddleeastinsider.com) — **do not rely on these specific numbers.**

## 5. How Authority Is Actually Built in KSA (HNAK's playbook)

The link types that move the needle in this market — all achievable for a mid-tier retailer:

1. 🟢 **Coupon / deal platforms** — high-DR, KSA-relevant, link *and* referral traffic. Priority targets: **AlCoupon (DR ~63, 5,100+ linking sites), CouponKSA (DR ~56), Almowafir (1,000+ partner stores), Picodi.** HNAK already appears on AlCoupon — expand systematically. *Sources: [Ahrefs alcoupon](https://ahrefs.com/websites/alcoupon.com), [Ahrefs couponksa](https://ahrefs.com/websites/couponksa.com), [Almowafir](https://www.linkedin.com/company/almowafir/)*
2. 🟢 **Price-comparison engines** — Pricena, Yaoota, etc. Feed accurate product/price data; these are persistent referral + link sources. HNAK is already on Pricena. *Source: [SimilarWeb comparison](https://www.similarweb.com/website/uae.alcoupon.com/vs/couponksa.com/)*
3. 🟢 **Regional editorial / digital PR** — Arab News, Saudi Gazette, Argaam, Mubasher, Zawya. Newsworthy angles (Al Musbah's 190+ store footprint, omnichannel, local sourcing) earn contextual Arabic links the pure-play competitors can't easily replicate. *Sources: [RankTracker KSA SEO](https://www.ranktracker.com/blog/a-complete-guide-for-doing-seo-in-saudi-arabia/), [Armanalahi Arabic link building](https://armanalahi.com/arabic-link-building-strategies/)*
4. 🟢 **Local directories & Google Business Profiles** — one per physical store → local-pack + "near me" + foundational citations. This is HNAK's **single biggest structural advantage** over online-only rivals. *Source: [RankTracker ME link building](https://www.ranktracker.com/blog/a-complete-guide-for-doing-link-building-in-the-middle-east/)*
5. 🟢 **Relevance > volume in Arabic link building** — niche-relevant Arabic links beat high-volume generic ones. *Source: [Armanalahi](https://armanalahi.com/arabic-link-building-strategies/)*

## 6. Prioritized Opportunities for HNAK

| **Digital PR around Al Musbah offline footprint** | Earns Arabic editorial links pure-plays can't | 🟢 |
